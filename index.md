Privacy Policy — Sweetch Timezone
Last updated: October 22, 2026
1. Introduction
Sweetch Timezone ("the app") is developed by Clément Trouillas. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.
2. Data collection
Sweetch Timezone does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you create in the app (events, alarms, time zone preferences, language settings) is stored exclusively on your device and, if enabled, in your personal iCloud account.
3. iCloud
If you choose to enable iCloud backup, your app data is stored in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple. Sweetch Timezone has no access to this data. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy
4. Third-party services
The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
5. Children's privacy
The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
6. Changes to this policy
Any updates to this policy will be reflected on this page with a new date.
7. Contact
For any questions: trouillas.clement@gmail.com
